# Isovist

Use [Ladybug analysis](../../ladybug-analysis/SKILL.md) for the selected observation points and heights, or reuse recorded results whose geometry and settings match. Follow the shared [map specification](map-specification.md) for framing and export.

Use one board per point/height pair with a common frame and scale. Draw the visible polygon in `#6FA8B8` at 35% opacity with a `#356B7A` boundary, 0.25 mm wide. Mark the eye position with a 2 mm white circle, dark outline, and point ID. Keep the context muted and site boundary visible; omit individual rays.

Use dashed edges where both endpoint rays reached the range limit. Key the visible region, observation point, and range limit. Label the eye height and reference, range, angular step, and field of view. Note when the view extends beyond the map frame. This is a horizontal view at eye height, not a ground-visibility map.

Check closed polygons, point/height labels, range marks, and alignment. Keep unresolved results separate from valid small regions. Report full-polygon area in m² and link the analysis record and CSV. Inspect the exported PDF at print size.
